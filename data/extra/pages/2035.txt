International Master of Research and Innovation (M.Sc.)
The International Master of Research and Innovation (IMRI) is a research-oriented, international master program in business psychology and business informatics.

Students have the opportunity to specialize in the fields of business psychology and business informatics. Alternatively, they can focus specifically on Human-Technology Interaction (HTI) as the connecting interface between people and technology. This allows them to develop a personalized academic profile.
A central element of the program is the independent exploration of real-world problems and complex research questions. In doing so, students develop innovative solutions by combining scientific methods with practical applications—often in collaboration with international partners, companies, or research institutions.
The program follows a project-based approach: Throughout the entire duration of their studies, students work on their own research or innovation project, either individually or in small teams. In doing so, they deepen their skills and build a professional and academic profile.
Graduates are prepared for careers in consulting, innovation, and research, as well as for further academic pursuits, such as a Ph.D. The program is taught primarily in English and can be pursued on either a full-time or part-time basis. Students can begin the program in either the winter or summer semester, which offers flexible opportunities for international experiences as well as for internships or professional work.

Course Content
In the full-time track, students combine subject-specific specialization with methodological and analytical training during the first two semesters, while also acquiring key competencies, such as teamwork and entrepreneurial thinking. This coursework is complemented by ongoing project work. In the third semester, students write their master’s thesis.
Depending on their chosen track—Business Psychology, Business Informatics, or Human-Technology Interaction (HTI)—students work on a subject-specific project and base their master’s thesis on it. In addition, each track offers relevant elective modules designed to support individual specialization.
In the part-time track, the program is structured over six semesters. The academic focus areas correspond to those of the full-time track but are spread out over a longer period.

Projects
A key feature of the IMRI program is its project-based approach: Students work continuously on their own research or innovation projects—either individually or in small teams—and in doing so develop a clear academic profile.

a scientific publication or research article
a conceptual approach or framework

Your perspective afterward
Graduates of the IMRI program acquire skills that are in demand across a variety of industries and fields of work. Specific career paths depend on individual interests and chosen areas of specialization. Typical fields of work include:
Research and Academic Careers
Positions in doctoral programs, research institutions, or corporate research—such as designing and conducting applied research projects, for example as a doctoral candidate or research analyst.
Innovation and product development
Roles in innovation management, product development, or research and development—with the goal of translating scientific findings into marketable solutions, such as an innovation manager or product manager.
Consulting, Strategy, and Expert Roles
Positions in strategy and transformation consulting—focused on developing evidence-based solutions for complex business challenges, for example as a transformation expert or a strategy or innovation consultant.
Your Application
Admission Requirements
The International Master of Research and Innovation master program is designed for applicants who hold a degree in Business Psychology or Business Informatics worth 210 ECTS credits. The Admissions Committee will determine the equivalence of other degrees.
If you have (almost) completed your degree but, for formal reasons, do not yet have a bachelor’s degree certificate at the time of application, you may still apply for a spot in the program. In this case, submit a confirmation (grade report, transcript of records, etc.) from your registrar’s office that
the credits earned at the time of graduation (210 or 180),
the individual grades from the completed modules, and
your current grade point average
.
Please note: If you do not have a diploma or confirmation stating your final grade, current grade point average, and ECTS credits earned, your application cannot be considered in the admissions process.
If you are also unable to submit the grade for your bachelor’s thesis at this time, your application materials must include a confirmation from your primary thesis advisor stating that the thesis will receive a grade of at least “satisfactory.”
For a bachelor’s degree requiring 180 ECTS credits, the remaining 30 ECTS credits must be submitted or earned during the first two semesters. This consists of an internship equivalent to a 20-week mandatory internship required by a degree program at a university of applied sciences, or professional work experience in a field relevant to the degree program. In each case, an internship or employment certificate must be submitted, including information on the duration, company division, and nature of the work. Another option is the successful completion of course modules related to Business Informatics, Business Psychology, or research-related fields at the Augsburg University of Applied Sciences.

Language Skills
Most modules in the program are taught in English; therefore, a minimum English proficiency level of B2 is recommended. Some courses may be taught in German. To be admitted, you must provide proof of German proficiency at the B2 level (Common European Framework of Reference for Languages). Assessment of Suitability and Admission
As part of the process to assess your suitability for the program, your application materials will be reviewed by the Admissions Committee and scored according to the criteria set forth in the syllabus and examination regulations.
The number of available spots is limited. Therefore, after the qualification requirements have been reviewed, offers of admission are extended to those applicants who achieved the highest scores in the scoring process (max. 105 points).
Applications from abroad
Applications from abroad are welcome! If you have completed your previous education abroad, please be sure to note that you must submit a preliminary review documentation (VPD) from “uni-assist e.V.” along with your application to the Technical University of Applied Sciences Augsburg. Processing your application through uni-assist can take up to 8 weeks. To ensure your application is not jeopardized, please submit your application to uni-assist well in advance so that you can also meet the application deadline for the master program itself. Further important information can be found in the guide for applying to master programs from abroad.

Application Period
Students may begin their studies in either the summer or winter semester. The application period for the summer semester of 2027 runs from December 1, 2026, to January 17, 2027. For the 2027–2028 winter semester, the application period begins on April 30, 2027, and runs through June 15, 2027.
Applications must be submitted to the Office of Academic Affairs at Augsburg University of Applied Sciences. Registration takes place via the THA application portal once the application period begins. Further information on the application and admission process at Augsburg University of Applied Sciences, as well as a list of answers to frequently asked questions, can be found on the THA website under “Questions About the Application.”
