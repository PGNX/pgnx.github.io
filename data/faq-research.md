# FAQ research (verified 2026-10-29)

Note: several official pages (auswaertiges-amt.de, make-it-in-germany.com, uni-assist glossary) could not be fetched or did not show the exact fact. Where only secondary sources support a claim, it is flagged.

## 1. Blocked account (Sperrkonto)
EUR 992 per month, so EUR 11,904 for the first year, is the figure in German embassy guidance and on DAAD ("992 euros per month, as of 1 January 2025"; DAAD also says amounts vary by purpose of stay, so check with the mission). Secondary sources say it is unchanged for 2026. Opening a blocked account costs roughly 70-150 EUR with the provider (DAAD). One outlier site (Studely) gave EUR 12,030; treat it as wrong. The Pakistan embassy student visa page itself does not state the amount (it links to a "financing" page).
Sources:
- https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/living-in-germany/finances/
- https://san-jose.diplo.de/resource/blob/1540562/fddac503242f24102b78bf7523dd7aba/merkblatt-studium-data.pdf (embassy leaflet: 992/month, 11,904 for one year)
- https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/2-study-visa-seite-1676104
Confidence: medium-high (amount consistent; no 2026-dated Pakistan-specific official page seen)

## 2. APS certificate for Pakistan
No official page found that requires APS for Pakistan. The APS requirement is widely reported to apply only to India, China, Vietnam and Mongolia, and none of the guides found list Pakistan. The Pakistan embassy student-visa page lists only degree certificates, transcripts and tuition proof as mandatory documents and does not mention APS. Two sources (ProPakistani, Vedantu) claim an APS office at the Islamabad embassy, but they are unsupported by any official page and conflict with the rest. Suggested FAQ wording: "APS is not currently required for Pakistani applicants; always confirm on the embassy page and with your university." Do not state an APS fee/time for Pakistan.
Sources:
- https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/2-study-visa-seite-1676104 (no APS mention)
- https://www.visaflow.app/blog/do-pakistanis-need-aps-certificate-germany (secondary)
- https://www.studienkolleg.org/en/blog/studienkolleg-guide-pakistani-students-complete/ (secondary)
- https://www.gradgermany.com/blog/aps-certificate-2026-guide-four-countries (secondary: four countries)
Confidence: medium (absence of requirement; no explicit official "Pakistan is exempt" statement)

## 3. uni-assist
uni-assist e.V. evaluates international applications for about 180 German universities (it does not work with commercial agencies and only issues payment requests itself via the My assist portal). Handling fee per semester: EUR 75 for the first chosen course, EUR 30 for each additional course (same for standard and VPD routes; non-refundable; EUR 10 admin fee on withdrawal after processing starts). A VPD (Vorprüfungsdokumentation) is a certificate uni-assist issues for certain universities, which you upload to the university; it lists your submitted certificates, how they were evaluated and your grade on the German scale. Processing is usually 4-6 weeks after submitting the paid online form; uni-assist advises applying at least 8 weeks before the deadline (Bayreuth says 6-8 weeks can happen). A VPD is valid for one year.
Sources:
- https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/pay-all-fees/handling-fees/
- https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/plan-your-application/vpd/
- https://www.uni-assist.de/en/
- https://www.studierendenkanzlei.uni-bayreuth.de/en/prospective-students/international-applicants/degree-students/uni-assist/index.html
Confidence: high (fee figures from search summary of uni-assist handling-fee page; VPD/time page fetched directly)

## 4. Student visa for Pakistani nationals
Applications go through the Federal Foreign Office Consular Services Portal (digital.diplo.de/studium): register, complete the questionnaire, upload documents, then a visa officer screens them and issues an appointment notification or asks for corrections. Islamabad covers Islamabad Capital Territory, Gilgit-Baltistan, Khyber Pakhtunkhwa, AJK and Punjab; Sindh and Balochistan are handled by Karachi. Mandatory uploads: school/bachelor/previous master certificates, transcripts, proof of tuition payment if applicable (plus admission letter, financial proof etc. per the questionnaire). The embassy says it is processing a high volume of student applications and suggests a late-arrival acceptance or next-semester enrolment letter. Fee EUR 75 (in PKR) and typical processing 5-10 weeks at Islamabad (4-6 for Karachi) came from an official-page snippet but were not on the fetched page. No VFS role for student visas was confirmed. Some guides report very long appointment waits (up to months), but no official figure was seen.
Sources:
- https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-en/service/2-study-visa-seite-1676104
- https://pakistan.diplo.de/pk-de/service/-/2370756
- https://www.dawn.com/news/amp/1854986 (reported appointment delays)
Confidence: medium (process and documents high; processing time and fee medium; waiting time low)

## 5. Public health insurance cost
TK 2026 for compulsory-insured students: EUR 110.38/month health insurance (including the TK additional contribution; Zusatzbeitrag 2.69%) plus nursing care insurance EUR 30.78 (under 23 or with a child) or EUR 35.91 (23+, childless), so about EUR 141-146 per month in total. Other public insurers (AOK, Barmer) are in the same range. DAAD uses an average of about EUR 100/month for health costs in a 2023 survey, which is out of date for 2026.
Sources:
- https://www.tk.de/resource/blob/2189784/b8633ce413c9374b641a41effadb8fdc/beratungsblatt-beitraege-der-tk-2026-data.pdf
- https://www.gesetzlichekrankenkassen.de/kassenbeitraege/studenten
- https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/living-in-germany/finances/
Confidence: medium-high (figures from search summaries of TK's 2026 sheet; recheck TK page before publishing exact numbers)

## 6. Semester contribution
DAAD: 70 to 430 EUR per semester, paid at enrolment and before each semester. It covers student services and student government (AStA) and, at many universities, a semester ticket for local public transport. It is separate from tuition.
Sources:
- https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/living-in-germany/finances/
Confidence: high

## 7. Tuition
Most public universities charge no tuition for Master's. Exceptions: Baden-Württemberg charges non-EU students EUR 1,500 per semester for Bachelor's and Master's (DAAD; doctoral students exempt; limited exemptions possible). Bavaria's 2023 law lets universities charge non-EU students; DAAD says fees can reach several thousand euros per semester and each institution sets its own. TUM is the main university using it, from winter semester 2024/25 for newly enrolling non-EU students; reported amounts (about EUR 2,000-3,000 Bachelor, 4,000-6,000 Master's per semester) conflict between sources and were not checked on a TUM page. Other states (e.g. NRW, Hesse, Saxony) charge only small or specific-case fees; not verified here.
Sources:
- https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/living-in-germany/finances/
- https://thepienews.com/tu-munchen-to-bring-in-international-fees-for-non-eu/
- https://www.gradgermany.com/blog/baden-wuerttemberg-non-eu-tuition-1500-sem
Confidence: high for BW and Bavaria in principle; low for exact TUM amounts

## 8. English proof
Common minimums are IELTS Academic 6.5 and TOEFL iBT about 88 (TUM Graduate Center and TUM Asia pages); some programmes (e.g. management) ask for more, unverified. TUM accepts an officially confirmed English-medium degree as proof in some programmes (check the programme page). Whether a Pakistani MOI letter is accepted depends on the programme; many universities accept MOI/English-taught degree confirmation, others require a test. No official page seen that says MOI is accepted universally.
Sources:
- https://mgc.mh.tum.de/it/node/3102
- https://tum-asia.edu.sg/?p=7100
- https://www.tum.de/fileadmin/user_upload_87/ge86zar/Infosession_How_Do_I_Apply_Master.pdf
Confidence: medium (generic; programme-specific)

## 9. GRE
Not universal, but important for Pakistani applicants. TUM: the Informatics and Data Engineering & Analytics Master's require GRE General (or GATE) for applicants whose degree is from India, Bangladesh, Pakistan, China or Iran, minimum Quant 164 and Analytical Writing 4.0 (Math in Data Science: 157 / 3.5). RWTH Aachen Computer Science Master's: GRE mandatory with percentile minimums (Quant above 75th, Verbal above 15th, AW at least 3.5); EU/EEA citizens exempt. TUM School of Management accepts GMAT only, not GRE. Saarland, Stuttgart: not verified on official pages. Check each programme page.
Sources:
- https://www.tum.de/en/studies/application/application-info-portal/special-conditions-for-certain-countries
- https://www.in.tum.de/en/in/for-prospective-students/apply-for-admission/masters-programs/
- https://sc.informatik.rwth-aachen.de/en/studium/master/informatik/start-ins-studium/
- https://www.mgt.tum.de/faq-center
Confidence: high for TUM and RWTH; low for others

## 10. Working while studying
Non-EU students may work 140 full days or 280 half days per year (half day = up to 4 hours; since 1 March 2024; counted per calendar year). Voluntary internships count; compulsory internships do not; student-assistant jobs at the university are generally outside the limit (sources vary); self-employment is not allowed. After graduating you can apply for a residence permit of up to 18 months to look for a job. These came from university pages (Mannheim, Heidelberg etc.) and a Make it in Germany search hit; the official Make it in Germany page was blocked.
Sources:
- https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/study-training/study/study-and-work/
- https://www.uni-mannheim.de/en/academics/coming-to-mannheim/international-students/visa-and-residence-permits/
- https://tu-freiberg.de/en/news/working-alongside-your-studies-new-regulations-march-1-2024
Confidence: medium-high

## 11. Grade conversion
uni-assist converts foreign grades to the German scale using the ZAB/KMK recommendations published in anabin. The modified Bavarian formula is named only in an older uni-assist service catalogue (2015/2020 PDF); no current uni-assist web page naming it was found, and country rules can override (e.g. Iran uses a stated Nmin). So "the modified Bavarian formula is what uni-assist uses" is widely true but not confirmed on a current page.
Sources:
- https://www.uni-assist.de/en/faqs/get-information/
- https://nutzertagung.uni-assist.de/fileadmin/Nutzertagung/Downloads/Leistungskatalog-uni_assist_03-2020.pdf
- https://www.uni-assist.de/en/how-to-apply/assemble-your-documents/educational-certificates/
Confidence: medium

## 12. Winter vs summer semester
DAAD: winter semester starts September/October, application typically early May to 15 July; summer semester starts March/April, application typically early December to 15 January. Many programmes differ (e.g. Regensburg CS 15 April-1 June for winter and 15 October-1 December for summer; some Master's close earlier, especially with uni-assist), so use the programme page. Recommended: start 8+ weeks before the deadline for uni-assist.
Sources:
- https://www.daad.de/en/studying-in-germany/requirements/application-process/
- https://www2.daad.de/deutschland/studienangebote/international-programmes/en/detail/9856/
Confidence: high for the typical pattern
