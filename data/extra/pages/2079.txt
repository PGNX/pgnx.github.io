The Master’s degree programme in Software Engineering (M.Sc.) provides advanced knowledge for the development and design of complex information systems as well as the processing of large amounts of data. The focus is on methodological and algorithmic competences that reflect the current state of research and enable the adaptation of existing models to different application contexts.
A wide range of electives allows the program to be flexibly tailored to individual interests and career goals. The Degree Programme imparts core competencies at both the technical and methodological levels. These enable students to cover the entire spectrum: from highly technical and programming-related aspects through advanced analysis and design techniques to methods and processes for coordinating and implementing large-scale software development projects. This is complemented by a team-oriented work style to continuously develop social skills.
The program builds on existing programming knowledge and aims to bring students up to an engineering level.

Admission Requirements
Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, information technology, computer engineering, mathematics, or an equivalent degree in a related Degree Programme
Strong analytical, creative, and logical competences, as well as good programming competences
Sufficient English proficiency is required for the Degree Programme. This can be demonstrated by a certificate (TOEFL Computer-based Test – min. 61 points, IELTS – min. 6, or an equivalent certificate) or a German high school diploma. If you previously studied in English, a document from the respective university is required certifying that the program was conducted entirely in English. This document must be stamped and signed by the university.
Further information on admission requirements can be found at the following link (electronic bulletin): Admission and Enrollment Regulations
Application and Admission
Application for Admission
The Software Engineering (M.Sc.) program currently has limited enrollment, which means that you must apply for admission. Applications and admissions are processed exclusively through the Faculty 4 application portal.
Admission Criteria
Admission is based on the following criteria:
Overall Grade of your bachelor’s degree (53%),
Number and grades of Bachelor’s courses in the field of Software Engineering (including mathematics and programming, 35%),
Previous research activities in the field of Software Engineering (6%)
previous practical experience in the field of software engineering (6%)
Application Documents
The following documents are required to apply for admission to the international Master’s degree programme in Software Engineering:
Copy of your Bachelor’s, Master’s, or diploma certificate, including a transcript of grades (all documents must be in English or accompanied by an English translation)
Language certificate for English (or equivalent)
Resume

During Studies
Course Structure and Course Catalogue
The two-year Master's degree course in Software Engineering comprises four semesters with a total of 120 credit points (CP). In terms of content, the course is divided into a methodological core area (65%), an application area (10%) and a Master's thesis (25%). The course structure varies depending on whether the course begins in the summer or winter term. Please note: "Method Specialization" and "Application" are placeholders where you can choose from the available course catalogue according to your interests.
Software Engineering/Methodology | min. 48 CP
(the three "Method Specialization" units can be selected individually according to the course catalogue | 6 CP each)
Software Engineering/Practice | min. 15 CP
(two exercises in the lab | 9 CP in total, one group project | 6 CP)
Software Engineering/Research | min. 15 CP
(one lab exercise | 3 CP, two projects | 12 CP in total)
"Application" | 12 CP
(the two "Application" units can be selected individually according to the course catalogue | 6 CP each)
Final examination | 30 CP
(Master's thesis 27 CP and final colloquium 3 CP)
The Master's Examination Regulations and the Course Catalogue Software Engineering M.Sc. provide information on the exact requirements and the courses that can currently be selected.

Related Degree Programs
Does the international Master's in Software Engineering sound exciting, but isn't quite the right fit? The international Master's in Data Analytics offers an attractive alternative - depending on your individual interests and career goals:
Data Analytics: the focus is on machine learning, big data and analysis technologies as well as the modeling and analysis of complex systems. The course is aimed in particular at people who want to expand their analytical skills and acquire knowledge of current analysis technologies in order to convert big data into meaningful business information. The course provides in-depth methodological and algorithmic knowledge in line with the current state of research. Graduates are able to apply their knowledge of machine learning in selected interdisciplinary application areas from business (e.g. marketing, logistics) and science (e.g. computer science, environmental sciences).

Career prospects
The Master's degree in Software Engineering qualifies students for the following - exemplary - professional fields:
Software Architect
Software Development Manager
Requirements Engineer
Software Developer
Product Manager
Consultant for Software Development
Our graduates work in these areas in a wide variety of application fields and conduct research in the field of Software Engineering on challenging topics.
Scientific Perspective and Doctoral Degree
Scientific work and the teaching of the underlying skills are an integral part of the Master's degree program. Students have the opportunity to participate in research projects during their studies, so that a successful career in research and science can already be embarked upon during the Master's degree.
A follow-up doctoral degree at the Institute of Computer Science offers a wide range of opportunities for independent scientific work and innovative research.
Anyone considering a doctoral degree will find excellent conditions and individual support at Faculty 4 of the University of Hildesheim.
