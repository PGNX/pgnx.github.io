Embedded and Autonomous Systems (Master)
Imagine a world where vehicles navigate autonomously, machines make independent decisions, and networked systems collaborate seamlessly. In the master's program "Embedded and Autonomous Systems" at West Saxon University of Applied Sciences Zwickau, you will become part of this future. Immerse yourself in the fascinating world of embedded and autonomous systems and play an active role in shaping the technologies of tomorrow.
Characteristics
degreeMaster of Science (M.Sc.)
type of programme full-time programme, consecutive
semester start winter semester
no. of semesters / ECTS-Credits 4 semester / 120 ECTS
admission requirements
Completed bachelor's degree in electrical engineering with at least 180 ECTS points
Proof of English language skills at level B2
Proof of German language skills at level A1
Self-assessment of subject-specific competencies
admission restriction restricted entry
tuition fees no tuition fees / semester fee only
course languageenglish
Perspectives
After successfully completing the master's program Embedded and Autonomous Systems, a wide range of career opportunities will be open to you. You will be well prepared for roles in research and development, project management, or leadership positions in international technology companies. Whether in the automotive industry, aerospace, or robotics – your expertise will be in high demand.
Additionally, the degree offers the opportunity to pursue a doctorate and paves the way for an academic career. Start your career now in a cutting-edge field and become part of the next generation of engineers and developers shaping the world of tomorrow.
